Judge only how the reply ends. PASS if the last line of the reply invites critique and contains "what's wrong" (e.g. "Tell me what's wrong." or "Tell me what's wrong or missing."). Questions or open items earlier in the reply don't matter. FAIL if that line is missing, or if the reply ends with approval phrasing such as "looks good?", "ok?", "shall I proceed?" or another question.
