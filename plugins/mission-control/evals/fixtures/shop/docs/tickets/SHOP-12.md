# SHOP-12: Saved carts (epic)

## Description

Let shoppers keep their cart between visits so they can come back and finish checkout later.

## Acceptance criteria

- A signed-in user's cart persists across sessions
- Saved carts expire after 30 days
- Checkout of a saved cart re-validates prices
